Guest-Ready Standard (10 points)

  1. Partnership form + rates on file; registered online
  2. Named ops contact + working 24h number tested
  3. Exterior + reception + ≥2 room photos (≤90 days)
  4. At least one room bookable with hourly / half-day rates
  5. Room clean, AC, bathroom, toiletries, linens
  6. Power backup confirmed and described honestly
  7. WiFi tested on-site
  8. Front-desk briefed on HPH check-in flow
  9. No unresolved safety or guest-surprise issues
  10. Test path completed (dummy booking walkthrough)
Only hotels passing all 10 items count as Live / Guest-Ready. Partial = Conditional.
